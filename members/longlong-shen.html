Biography

Longlong Shen received his B.E. degree in Digital Media Technology from Shanxi Datong University. He is currently a Master’s student in Computer Technology at the School of Information Science and Technology, Baotou Teachers’ College, advised by Dr. Axida Shan. His research focuses on federated learning for vehicular networks.

Research Interests

  • Communication compression techniques for federated learning
  • Fair aggregation algorithms for federated learning
  • Federated class-incremental learning
