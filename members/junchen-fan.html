Biography

Junchen Fan received his B.E. degree from Hebei University, Baoding, China. He is currently pursuing a master's degree at Hohhot Minzu College, China, under the supervision of Dr. Axida Shan. His research focuses on semantic communications and adaptive switching between semantic and conventional codecs.

Research Interests

  • Semantic communications and joint source-channel coding
  • Rate–distortion analysis and adaptive switching between semantic and conventional codecs
