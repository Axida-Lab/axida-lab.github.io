Biography

Axida Shan (Member, IEEE) received the B.S. degree in computer networking from Inner Mongolia University, Hohhot, China, in 2004, the M.S. degree in information network system from The University of Electro-Communications, Tokyo, Japan, in 2010, and the Ph.D. degree in control science and engineering from Xi’an University of Technology, Xi’an, China, in 2023. He is currently an Associate Professor with Baotou Teachers’ College, Baotou, China. He was a Visiting Scholar at The University of Electro-Communications from July 2023 to June 2024.

Research Interests

  • Vehicular networks and vehicular edge computing
  • Federated learning and incentive mechanisms
  • Semantic communications and edge intelligence
  • Cooperative computing and reinforcement learning

Teaching

Teaching areas include C Programming, Data Structures and Algorithms, Artificial Intelligence, Python Programming, and Machine Learning.
