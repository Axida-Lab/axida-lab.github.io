About Axida Lab

An academic research group working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, communication networks, and distributed computing.

OUR MISSION

Researching the intelligence behind connected systems.

Axida Lab investigates how intelligent algorithms and networked systems can work together in dynamic and resource-constrained environments. Our research combines machine learning, communication technologies, optimization, and incentive mechanisms.

We are particularly interested in connected vehicles, the Internet of Things, and edge computing, where devices must make timely decisions while sharing limited communication and computing resources.

Research approach

We develop algorithmic mechanisms, build simulation-based evaluation environments, and study the performance, robustness, and efficiency of collaborative intelligent systems.
