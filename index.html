01 / ABOUT

Researching the intelligence
behind connected worlds.

Axida Lab is an academic research group focusing on intelligent computing and communication networks. We investigate how learning algorithms, distributed systems, and incentive mechanisms can enable efficient and reliable collaboration among connected devices.

Our work brings together artificial intelligence, networking, and optimization, with applications in vehicular networks, the Internet of Things, and edge computing.
